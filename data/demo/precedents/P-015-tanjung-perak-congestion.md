> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** Vessel congestion at Tanjung Perak in Surabaya delayed loading of containers bound for Makassar by about three days. A distributor in South Sulawesi had about four days of cover for cooking oil.

**Action.** The planner did not buy alternate transport. The distributor was informed of the new arrival date and agreed to slow promotions for a week. The next two shipments were booked earlier to rebuild cover.

**Cost and outcome.** No direct cost. A small loss of promotional sales, estimated at Rp 7M.

**Lesson.** For long inter-island lanes, keep the distributor informed early. Expediting is rarely worth it when the customer's buffer covers most of the delay.
