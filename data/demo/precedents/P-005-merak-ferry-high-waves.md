> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** High waves in the Sunda Strait suspended ferry crossings between Merak and Bakauheni for about 36 hours. Outbound trucks from Cikarang DC to Lampung and Palembang distributors queued at Merak port.

**Action.** The planner informed the two Sumatra distributors and moved their delivery dates by two days. Trucks stayed in the queue rather than returning, to keep their place for the first crossings. A request to send stock by air was declined because distributors held about five days of cover.

**Cost and outcome.** Driver waiting allowance of Rp 4.8M. No customer penalty because both distributors had buffer stock.

**Lesson.** Before buying alternate transport, check the customer's own stock cover. Waiting is often the cheapest option when the downstream buffer covers the delay.
