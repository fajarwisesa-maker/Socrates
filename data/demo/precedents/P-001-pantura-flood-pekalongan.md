> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** Flooding near Pekalongan closed Jalur Pantura for heavy trucks. An FTL of 1,000 cartons of 2L cooking oil from a Semarang supplier, bound for Cikarang DC, was held for about 60 hours. Two customer orders loading the next evening were at risk.

**Action.** The planner moved 4 trucks of stock from Bandung DC, taking Bandung almost to zero, and bought a small bridge quantity from a Jabodetabek supplier. Air charter was quoted but not used.

**Cost and outcome.** Transfer Rp 7.6M, bridge premium Rp 6.2M. Both Cikarang orders shipped on time. Two days later Bandung DC could not fill its own wholesale orders and paid Rp 45M in lost margin.

**Lesson.** Never take a donor DC below its safety stock. Cap the transfer at stock above safety stock and buy the remainder from the alternate supplier, even at a higher premium.
