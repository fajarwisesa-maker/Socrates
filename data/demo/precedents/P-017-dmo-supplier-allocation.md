> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** A new domestic market obligation rule for cooking oil led the primary supplier to cut allocations to branded customers by 20 percent for a month. Cikarang DC faced a gradual shortfall of about 1,500 cartons over four weeks.

**Action.** The planner split the shortfall between two approved alternate suppliers, each within its stated capacity, and spread the orders weekly. Each new purchase order was approved by the procurement lead.

**Cost and outcome.** Premiums totalling Rp 21M over the month. No customer penalties.

**Lesson.** Respect each alternate supplier's capacity and split volumes rather than overloading one. A slow, predictable shortfall should be handled with planned orders, not emergency measures.
