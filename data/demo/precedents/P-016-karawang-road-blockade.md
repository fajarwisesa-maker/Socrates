> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** A protest blocked an arterial road near Karawang for about eight hours. Driver messages on WhatsApp reported long queues, and a forwarder warned of possible delays to Cikarang deliveries.

**Action.** The planner checked open orders and found that all inbound trucks could arrive before the next loading cutoff even with an eight-hour delay. No mitigation was bought. The case was logged and closed after the road reopened.

**Cost and outcome.** No cost. All orders shipped on time.

**Lesson.** Not every disruption signal needs action. Compare the realistic delay with the next cutoff before spending money. Logging the case still helps build the precedent base.
