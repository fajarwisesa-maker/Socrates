> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** Before year end the regular forwarder on the Semarang–Jakarta lane could supply only half the requested trucks. Three FTLs of cooking oil were waiting at the supplier.

**Action.** The planner booked two spot trucks from another carrier at a 35 percent premium and asked the supplier to hold the third load for two days. The forwarder was asked to give advance notice of capacity limits in future.

**Cost and outcome.** Spot premium Rp 4.4M. All loads arrived before the stock ran out at Cikarang DC.

**Lesson.** Carrier capacity is a disruption signal just like weather. Spot trucks are usually cheaper than air or bridge buying when the delay comes from capacity, not from road access.
