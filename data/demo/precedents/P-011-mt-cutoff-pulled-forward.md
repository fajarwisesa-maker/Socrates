> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** A modern-trade retailer moved its loading cutoff 12 hours earlier for a promotion. Cikarang DC was short 300 cartons against the new cutoff, although stock would arrive in time for the original one.

**Action.** Any change to a customer service agreement needed approval from the key account manager. The planner prepared two options: a transfer from Bandung DC, or a request to keep the original cutoff. The account manager chose the transfer and confirmed the new cutoff in writing.

**Cost and outcome.** Transfer Rp 3.9M. The retailer accepted the delivery on time.

**Lesson.** SLA changes go to a human, every time. Prepare the cost of each option so the account manager can decide quickly.
