> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** The government restricted trucks with three or more axles on major Java toll roads and arterials for nine days around Lebaran. Inbound replenishment to Jabodetabek DCs would stop while demand for cooking oil and sugar peaked.

**Action.** Because the restriction was announced three weeks ahead, the planner pre-built stock: two extra weeks of cover at Cikarang and Bandung DCs, with deliveries scheduled before the restriction began. Small two-axle trucks, which were exempt, covered emergency top-ups.

**Cost and outcome.** Extra storage and handling of about Rp 22M and two small-truck runs at Rp 3.1M. No stockouts during the restriction.

**Lesson.** Known regulatory restrictions are planning problems, not disruptions. Pre-build early and keep exempt vehicle capacity on call. Do not treat a scheduled restriction as force majeure with customers.
