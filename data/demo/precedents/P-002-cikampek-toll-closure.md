> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** A structural inspection after a tanker fire closed the Jakarta–Cikampek elevated toll and the lanes below it for about 20 hours. Inbound trucks from Bandung and Cirebon to Cikarang DC queued on arterial roads. A modern-trade order and a wholesale order were due to load the same evening.

**Action.** The planner protected the modern-trade order, which had an OTIF penalty, using stock already on hand at Cikarang. The wholesale customer agreed to take their order one day late. No extra transport was bought because the road was expected to reopen within the day.

**Cost and outcome.** Rp 12M margin concession to the wholesale customer. No contractual penalty. The road reopened after 19 hours.

**Lesson.** For short closures with a credible reopening time, reschedule the lower-penalty order rather than paying for new transport. Get the customer's consent in writing before changing the delivery date.
