> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** A dock workers' strike at Tanjung Priok stopped container release for four days. Imported milk powder for a modern-trade promotion was stuck in two containers. Shortfall at Cikarang DC was about 900 cartons against a promotion start date with a high penalty.

**Action.** Air freight from Singapore was quoted at Rp 85M and rejected as out of proportion. The planner bought 900 cartons from an approved local alternate supplier at a premium of Rp 18,000 per carton. The purchase was a new external commitment and went to the supply chain head for approval.

**Cost and outcome.** Premium Rp 16.2M. Approval took 35 minutes. Promotion started on time; the stuck containers were absorbed into the next month's plan.

**Lesson.** Check approved local alternates before air. Air freight only makes sense when no ground option covers the shortfall before the cutoff.
