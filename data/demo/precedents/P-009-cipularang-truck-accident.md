> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** A truck carrying 250 cartons from Bandung DC to Cikarang DC overturned on the Cipularang toll. The driver was unhurt but the load was written off. The cartons were part of a planned transfer to cover a modern-trade order.

**Action.** The planner sent a second truck from Bandung the same afternoon, after checking that Bandung would stay above its safety stock. The insurer was notified within 24 hours with photos and the police report.

**Cost and outcome.** Second truck Rp 1.95M. The insurance claim recovered the cargo value two months later. The order loaded on time.

**Lesson.** Internal transfers are reversible and cheap, but each truck is a single point of failure. Keep the donor DC's safety stock intact so a repeat transfer is still possible.
