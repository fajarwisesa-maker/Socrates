> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** A batch of 2L cooking oil from the primary supplier failed the free fatty acid test at goods receipt in Cikarang DC. 800 cartons were blocked and returned. Replacement from the same supplier would take five days.

**Action.** The planner ordered 600 cartons from an approved alternate supplier with a one-day lead time and used 200 cartons of Cikarang stock above safety stock. The alternate's quotation was EXW, so the planner asked for DDP to avoid arranging a separate truck; the supplier agreed for a small surcharge.

**Cost and outcome.** Premium Rp 9.6M plus Rp 1.2M for delivery. All orders shipped. The primary supplier credited the failed batch.

**Lesson.** Check the Incoterm on bridge orders. An EXW quote looks cheaper but leaves transport, risk and timing with us.
