> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** An eruption spread ash over central Java and several airports closed for two days. At the same time a supplier truck from Solo was delayed. The planner's first plan relied on an air charter to cover a shortfall at Cikarang DC.

**Action.** The charter was cancelled because the departure airport was closed. The planner switched to a stock transfer from Bandung DC and a partial bridge order from a Jabodetabek supplier, and asked one wholesale customer to accept a split delivery.

**Cost and outcome.** Transfer Rp 3.9M, bridge premium Rp 4.5M, no penalties.

**Lesson.** Air freight depends on airports, slots and weather. Confirm that the air option is actually available before presenting it, and always keep a ground fallback in the plan.
