> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** A new 1L cooking-oil pack was due to launch with a modern-trade chain. The inbound truck from Semarang was delayed by a road collapse, and the contract carried a Rp 300M penalty for missing the launch date. No other DC held the new pack and no alternate supplier could make it.

**Action.** With no ground option, the planner requested an air charter. As a spot air shipment it needed the supply chain director's approval, which was given after 40 minutes.

**Cost and outcome.** Air charter Rp 28M. The launch went ahead on time.

**Lesson.** Air freight is the right choice when the penalty is large and no ground option exists. Make the approval request clear and fast: what, why, cost and alternatives.
