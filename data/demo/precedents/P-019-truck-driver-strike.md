> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** Truck drivers in several Java cities stopped work for two days in protest against new overloading enforcement. Some forwarders on Pantura and the Cikampek toll could not run their fleets.

**Action.** The planner checked which inbound shipments were affected and found most trucks were already loaded and parked. Shipments resumed after the strike ended. For one urgent modern-trade order, a transfer from Bandung DC using the company's own contracted trucks covered the gap.

**Cost and outcome.** Transfer Rp 1.95M. Other deliveries arrived one to two days late with no penalty.

**Lesson.** Labour disruptions affect carriers unevenly. Check which carriers are actually stopped before assuming the whole lane is closed.
