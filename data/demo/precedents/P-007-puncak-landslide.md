> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** Heavy rain caused a landslide on the Bogor–Puncak–Cianjur road. Deliveries from Cikarang DC to retailers in Cianjur and Sukabumi could not use the usual route. The road authority estimated three days to clear one lane.

**Action.** The planner rerouted trucks through the Cipularang toll and Padalarang. The longer route added about four hours per trip, so one extra truck was hired for the week to keep the delivery schedule.

**Cost and outcome.** Extra truck hire Rp 8.4M and fuel of about Rp 2.5M. All deliveries arrived within the customers' windows.

**Lesson.** When a reroute exists, compare its added cost and time with the delivery window before considering other options. A reroute keeps the same stock plan and needs no new supplier commitments.
