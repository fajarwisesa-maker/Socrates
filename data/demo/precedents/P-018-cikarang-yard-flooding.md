> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** Heavy overnight rain flooded the truck yard at Cikarang DC. Trucks could not reach the docks for about ten hours, so inbound and outbound loads both stopped.

**Action.** The DC prioritised the modern-trade load with the earliest cutoff once water levels fell. Two wholesale customers agreed to collect their orders the next morning. Inbound trucks waited at a nearby parking area until the yard was cleared and safe for unloading.

**Cost and outcome.** Waiting charges of Rp 3.6M. No contractual penalties.

**Lesson.** When the destination DC itself is affected, transfers into it do not help. Prioritise outbound loads by penalty and cutoff and talk to customers early.
