> **SYNTHETIC** precedent written for the SIAGA hackathon demo. Companies are fictional and costs are illustrative.

**Situation.** Demand for 2L cooking oil in Jabodetabek rose about 40 percent above forecast in the first week of Ramadan. All DCs were drawing down safety stock and wholesale customers were placing larger orders than usual.

**Action.** The planner applied a fair-share allocation: modern-trade orders with contractual penalties were filled first, and wholesale orders were capped at their average weekly volume. Extra production was requested from the primary supplier for the following week.

**Cost and outcome.** Some wholesale margin was lost, estimated at Rp 30M. No contractual penalties. Safety stock was rebuilt within ten days.

**Lesson.** In a demand spike, allocation rules are a business decision. The planner should apply the agreed rules and escalate any exception rather than improvising.
